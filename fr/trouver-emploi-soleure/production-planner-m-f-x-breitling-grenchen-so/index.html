Description complète

We are a dedicated Squad on a mission to redefine Swiss watchmaking, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision we create timepieces that combine modern-retro design with casual, approachable, and sustainable luxury — powered by passionate teams around the world. Join our vibrant team in Grenchen as a Production Planner and be part of something extraordinary! We are currently looking for a new team member to join our Squad! It’s more than just a Production Planner role - it’s an opportunity to have a strategic impact on the effective coordination and prioritization of production activities. The role also involves leading the planning and monitoring of new product introductions to ensure successful launches and a smooth industrial ramp-up. Your main mission will be to develop, maintain, and optimize the production plan within a 0-to-4-month planning horizon.

Your contribution: Production Planning & Execution Management (0–4 Months Horizon):

  • You develop, maintain and optimize feasible and executable production plans for Watch Heads, Watches and Accessories within a 0-to-4-month planning horizon, ensuring the effective deployment of S&OP decisions.
  • You manage detailed T2 production planning across both internal and subcontracted operations.
  • You balance demand, capacity, material availability and operational constraints to achieve service, inventory and business objectives.
  • You ensure short-term capacity and workload management, identifying bottlenecks and defining mitigation actions when required.
  • You ensure production priorities are aligned with business objectives, customer requirements and operational capabilities.
  • You act as the primary planning interface with T2/T3 Production Management and coordinate planning decisions across production flows.
  • You facilitate short- and mid-term planning reviews, prepare planning scenarios and support decision-making through impact analyses and planning simulations.
  • You drive cross-functional alignment on production priorities and operational execution. New Product Launch Tracking:
  • You lead the planning and monitoring of new product introductions to ensure successful launches.
  • You ensure production capacity and material availability are aligned with launch milestones and ramp-up requirements.
  • You monitor component availability, planning risks and launch-related constraints, defining mitigation plans when required.
  • You provide regular reporting on launch progress, key milestones, readiness status and potential issues.
  • You support decision-making and prioritization activities to secure launch commitments and achieve business objectives. Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement:
  • You monitor planning performance indicators and identify opportunities to improve planning effectiveness and operational performance.
  • You analyze the root causes of production plan deviations and implement corrective and preventive actions.
  • You track production plan adherence, schedule attainment and planning accuracy, driving continuous performance improvement.
  • You develop recommendations to improve production stability, capacity utilization and service performance.
  • You contribute to the continuous improvement of planning processes, systems, tools and master data quality. Do you think there is even more to discover in this role? You're right — apply today and learn more! You will fit perfectly into our Squad if:
  • You have experience in Production Planning or a similar role within a manufacturing environment, ideally in the watchmaking industry (considered a plus).
  • You have a solid understanding of industrial operations and production planning processes.
  • You hold a Master's degree in Engineering, Supply Chain Management or a related discipline.
  • You may also have an equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience.
  • You have a solid understanding of Planning and S&OP processes.
  • You are proficient in SAP, particularly in MRP, Production Orders, Inventory and Sales Orders.
  • You have advanced Excel skills.
  • You are proficient in PowerPoint.
  • You are comfortable using Power BI and have strong data analysis skills.
  • You speak French and English fluently.
  • Your experience or any exposure to SAP IBP will be considered a plus.
  • You demonstrate a strong analytical mindset and a structured approach to problem-solving. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
